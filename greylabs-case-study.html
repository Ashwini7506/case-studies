02 — Background

What Is PIVC?

A mandatory cost the insurance industry has to pay on every single policy. It does not generate revenue. It does not improve the product. It exists because the regulator says so.

Definition

Pre-Issuance Verification Call

Before any life insurance policy above a premium threshold is issued, IRDAI requires the insurer to call the customer and confirm they understood what they bought — premium, term, surrender rules, cooling-off period.

Why it exists

Stop Mis-Selling

Insurance agents have historically mis-sold policies to customers who did not understand terms. PIVC is IRDAI's check. If customer cannot confirm understanding, the policy does not get issued.

Who pays

The Insurer. 100%.

PIVC is an operational expense. Call centre, agents, recording infrastructure, compliance review. None of this is billed back to the customer. It is pure overhead on every policy sold.

Business reality

Regulatory Tax, Not Product

No insurer wants to do PIVC. They have to. Every minute of a PIVC call is a cost with zero customer lifetime value lift. Whoever makes PIVC cheaper and safer owns a large, captive B2B opportunity.

Simple framing — PIVC is a fixed cost every Indian life insurer pays, every day, on every policy. GreyLabs' opportunity is to compress that cost from ₹25-40 per call to single-digit rupees, while making compliance stronger than a human-run process.

03 — Cost Problem

The Size of the Bill

PIVC at industry scale is a crore-rupees-per-month line item on every large insurer's P&L. And most of it is still humans on phones.

Volume

~40-60M PIVC Calls a Year

India issued ~30M new life policies in FY25 across LIC, HDFC Life, SBI Life, ICICI Prudential, Tata AIA, Max Life and others. Each policy over the PIVC threshold triggers one call, often with retries — ballpark 40-60M calls per year industry-wide.

Unit cost

₹25-40 per Call

Human agent, 8-12 minutes of scripted reading, supervisor oversight, recording, audit sampling. For a large insurer issuing 1M policies a year, that is ~₹25-40 Cr/year on one compliance process.

Industry exposure

₹1,500-2,500 Cr/Year

Rough industry spend on PIVC operations annually. 100% of this is regulatory cost, not product. Every paisa saved flows straight to the insurer's bottom line.

Regulatory tail

Fines Beyond the Call Cost

IRDAI has been actively penalising mis-selling since 2024. A single mis-sold policy traced back to a failed PIVC can cost the insurer ₹1 Cr+ in fines, plus refund liability and brand damage.

The question for GreyLabs: If you can cut per-call cost from ₹25-40 to ₹5-8 while making compliance audit-ready by default, how much of that ₹1,500-2,500 Cr industry spend is addressable — and how fast can you capture it?

08 — Solution

Staged Autonomy

Not every PIVC call should be fully autonomous. Stage the automation by customer risk profile and call complexity.

Stage 1 — Simple

Fully autonomous

Standard term policy, customer answered warm-up. AI runs the full PIVC. ~60% of calls.

Stage 2 — Adaptive

AI + soft check-in

Older customer, regional language, ULIP or annuity product. AI adapts pace, adds comprehension checks. ~25% of calls.

Stage 3 — Hybrid

AI opens, human closes

High-premium policy or customer showing confusion. AI handles disclosures, human agent confirms. ~10% of calls.

Stage 4 — Exception

Route to human

Customer agitated, using escalation keywords, or grievance-shaped. Straight to a certified human agent. ~5% of calls.

The shift — "all humans" or "all AI" is the wrong frame. Stage autonomy by risk. AI handles the 60% where the script is clean. Humans handle the 5% where judgment is non-negotiable. Everything in between is a hybrid.

09 — Feature

Warm-Up Pre-Call

The single biggest lever on first-attempt completion — a 2-hour heads-up. Costs almost nothing, changes the answer rate.

  1. Policy issued → trigger schedules PIVC for T+24h at a predicted best-call time per customer.
  2. 2 hours before call — WhatsApp template drops: "Your HDFC Life policy #12345 is ready. We'll call in 2 hours to confirm you understand the terms. Takes 5 minutes."
  3. Message includes a one-tap "reschedule" button. Customer can move the slot without a human.
  4. Call fires. Customer is expecting it. Pickup rate lifts.
  5. If no-answer, system reschedules using time-of-day model — not blind retry.

Answer Rate Lifts ~20 pts

Warm-up + one-tap reschedule brings first-attempt pickup from ~50% to ~70% based on cold-vs-warm outbound benchmarks.

Biggest single lever

Near-Zero Cost

WhatsApp Business template at ~25 paise per message. ~₹0.25 to save a ~₹25 failed call.

100x ROI on warm-up

Builds Trust

Customer is not surprised. They feel informed, not ambushed. Better engagement through the whole call.

Trust win

Shrinks Ops Queue

Fewer retries means the insurer's ops team stops drowning in "no answer" cases.

Ops cost drop
10 — Feature

Comprehension Checkpoints

Compliance needs more than a "yes". This feature turns a passive confirmation into a live check the customer actually understood.

  1. AI reads disclosure (example — cooling-off period).
  2. Instead of "do you understand?", AI asks a micro-question in the customer's language: "If you cancel in the first 15 days, what happens to your money?"
  3. Speech-to-text interprets answer. Correct paraphrase in any language passes.
  4. If answer is wrong or "I don't know", AI explains once more in simpler words and asks again.
  5. After two failed attempts, mark this disclosure as "needs human" and continue the rest of the call.

Real Comprehension, Not Fake Yes

Customer who says yes without understanding now fails the checkpoint. That is the exact mis-selling pattern IRDAI is chasing.

Regulatory win

Skip for Confident Customers

Customer who paraphrases correctly on the first checkpoint — reduce frequency on later disclosures. Short call for confident users.

UX win

Escalation Signal

Two failed checkpoints = flag for human follow-up. Do not fake-pass the PIVC. Fewer complaints 6 months later.

Compliance signal

This is the single feature that would change GreyLabs from "fast voice agent" to "safer voice agent than a human." A human agent reads monotonously. An AI that actually checks comprehension is structurally better at catching mis-selling.

11 — Feature

Structured Compliance Audit Trail

The piece that lets the insurer's compliance head sleep. Every disclosure, every customer response, logged as structured data — not just audio.

  1. Before call — pull the current regulatory requirements from a central policy DB (IRDAI circulars, insurer-specific clauses). Agent never holds regulatory text statically.
  2. During call — every required disclosure tagged with timestamp, customer response verbatim, comprehension check result.
  3. End of call — generate a structured report: 10/10 disclosures covered, 9/10 comprehension checks passed, 1 flagged for human review.
  4. Compliance head dashboard — every call searchable by pass/fail, by customer, by policy type. Can pull an audit-ready PDF in one click.
  5. When IRDAI updates rules (eg. DPDP Act 2026), compliance team updates central DB once. All agents across all insurers pull the new version automatically.

Defensible to Regulator

Compliance can hand IRDAI a per-call audit trail. Not a 10-minute audio file — a structured confirmation of every disclosure.

Buyer decision lever

Zero Static Regulatory Text

Model never answers regulatory questions from memory. All policy text comes from a live DB. Fix a rule centrally — every call follows the new rule next day.

Prevents drift

Sales Unlock

This is the feature that moves the compliance head from "block" to "champion" — turns GreyLabs from a tech vendor into a compliance partner.

GTM unlock

13 — Prioritisation

What to Ship First

Quick wins unlock the feature. Strategic bets scale it. Stay honest about what is a quarter and what is a year.

Quick Wins — Low Effort, High Impact

  • Warm-up pre-call — WhatsApp template + scheduling slot. 2-3 week build. Biggest lever on completion rate.
  • Time-of-day retry model — Predict best call slot per customer. Small ML model on call history. 3-4 weeks.
  • Basic compliance log — Tag every disclosure with timestamp + customer response. Config change, not a new product.

Strategic Bets — High Effort, High Impact

  • Comprehension checkpoints — Micro-question + multilingual paraphrase matching. 8-12 weeks. This is the compliance moat.
  • Staged autonomy routing — Rule engine + risk model to decide fully-AI vs hybrid vs human per call. 8-10 weeks.
  • Central policy DB + live pull — Compliance team updates rules once, every agent follows. 10-12 weeks.

Fill-ins — Low Effort, Lower Impact

  • Post-call summary SMS — Summary of what customer confirmed. 1 sprint. Nice trust signal.
  • Agent performance dashboard — Internal analytics for insurer ops head. Existing Speech Analytics can be extended.

Avoid — High Effort, Low Impact

  • Full emotion AI model — Expensive to build, low marginal lift over keyword-based escalation triggers.
  • Video PIVC — Does not match customer device reality in Tier-2/3 India. Audio-first is right.
14 — Risks

What Could Go Wrong

Each feature has an edge case that kills it if ignored. Name it, measure it, mitigate it.

Customer Fails Comprehension They Actually Understood

False negative on a checkpoint annoys the customer and adds friction. Mitigation: Paraphrase matching via LLM-as-judge with a manual review sample. Two-chance policy.

Precision risk

IRDAI Rejects AI-Only PIVC

Regulator may insist on human-in-loop for any AI PIVC. Mitigation: Lead with hybrid. Build compliance head as champion. Share audit trail with IRDAI early for informal sign-off.

Regulatory risk

Language Model Drift

Accuracy drops on regional languages as usage shifts across insurers. Mitigation: Per-insurer eval set of 100 gold-standard calls. Weekly drift check. Auto-flag for retraining.

Model risk

Agent Channel Pushback

Insurance agents may push back — they prefer coaching customers through a human PIVC to protect commission. Mitigation: Ship agent-facing dashboard showing faster commission payout with AI PIVC. Align incentives.

Channel risk

Every acceleration lever adds a new failure mode. The product job is to find the calibration point — fast enough for ops to adopt, safe enough for compliance to sign off, honest enough that the customer is never mis-sold.

15 — Summary

The Playbook

Do not try to fully autonomise PIVC overnight. Stage it. Win the compliance head first, not the ops head. Build the audit trail as hard as you build the voice.
