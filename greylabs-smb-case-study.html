02 — Problem

BFSI Is Not the Biggest Market

The same technology GreyLabs ships to banks could serve 10-15 million small Indian businesses — but the current motion cannot reach a single one of them.

Who is being left out

The Entire Indian SMB Economy

Clinics, retail shops, tuition centres, local service providers, salons, travel agents, hotels, real-estate brokers. They all handle customer calls manually. They all want AI. None of them can buy GreyLabs today.

Why they are locked out

Three Structural Walls

Enterprise pricing — minimum ₹50 L contracts are impossible for SMBs. Custom implementation — a 6-week build does not fit a ₹10 lakh/year business. Integration complexity — an SMB has no engineering team to wire up APIs.

What it costs them

Manual Call Handling

Shopkeepers miss calls while serving customers. Clinics double-book appointments. Tuition centres chase fee reminders by hand. Service providers lose leads by not calling back fast enough.

What it costs GreyLabs

An Order-of-Magnitude Larger TAM

BFSI addressable revenue caps in the low thousand crores. The SMB layer underneath it is tens of thousands of crores — and entirely unserved by agentic voice AI today.

The product question: How does GreyLabs reach the SMB tier without cannibalising the enterprise motion, without collapsing unit economics, and without a 6-week onboarding for every new customer?

Two years ago this was not a shippable bet — digital POS was too thin, mobile usage too patchy, and SMBs had not yet encountered AI. All three gates are open now.

06 — The Gap

There Is No Connector Layer for SMB Voice AI

Enterprise voice AI exists (Gnani, Observe.AI, GreyLabs today). SMB digital software exists (Khatabook, Paytm Business, Dukaan). Nobody sits between them.

The Composio analogy

Composio bundled 1,000+ MCPs behind one CLI. Developers stopped writing OAuth flows, API wrappers, or integration glue. They added Composio and got access to every connected tool through one layer.

The unlock: Composio's value is not the individual tool — it is the bundling. Each tool alone was always buildable. The layer is what made them accessible in one motion.

What SMBs need, same shape

Bundle voice AI + speech analytics + email analytics + POS customer data behind one connector. The SMB owner does not write a single line of integration code. They click a toggle in their POS, grant OAuth scopes, and an AI voice agent goes live for their business.

The unlock: GreyLabs's value becomes the layer, not the underlying models. Competitors can build voice AI. They cannot build the layer without rebuilding the POS relationships.

The gap is not a missing feature. It is a missing distribution layer. The company that builds this layer owns SMB voice AI for the next decade.

07 — Solution

GreyLabs Connect

A self-serve integration layer that wires GreyLabs's Voice, Speech and Email AI into the Indian SMB stack. Go-live in under an hour, no engineering required.

What it is

A Connector, Not a New Product

Connect is a thin OAuth + webhook + config layer that sits on top of GreyLabs's existing AI stack. The underlying models, voice infrastructure, and analytics are the same ones serving BFSI today.

Pre-built integrations

Top 8 Indian SMB Platforms

Khatabook, Paytm for Business, Dukaan, Dotpe, Shopify India, WhatsApp Business API, Google Calendar, Zoho Books. Cover the majority of digital-first SMBs in one release.

Pricing

Usage-Based, No Lock-In

₹5-8 per AI-handled call. ₹0.25 per AI-drafted email. Free tier: 50 calls/month. No minimum commit, no contracts. Cancel anytime.

Go-live experience

Click → OAuth → Live in < 1 Hour

Owner toggles "AI Voice Agent" inside their POS. OAuth flow grants GreyLabs scoped access. Business-type template auto-loaded. Owner makes a test call. Production.

09 — Technical Layer

How to Actually Build This

Under the hood — auth, event contract, AI layer, cost routing, feedback. Enough detail that an engineering lead could start sizing.

Step 1 · Auth

OAuth 2.0 with Scoped Permissions

Each POS exposes an OAuth 2.0 app. Connect requests scopes: read:customers, read:orders, write:interactions. Refresh tokens stored server-side, encrypted at rest. No customer PII leaves POS unless call context requires it.

Step 2 · Event ingress

Webhook Contract

POS POSTs a signed webhook to /connect/events whenever something happens (new order, cart abandon, missed call, reminder due). Idempotency key + HMAC signature verification. Queue-backed ingestion so POS never waits for GreyLabs processing.

POST /connect/events X-GreyLabs-Signature: sha256=... { "event_id": "evt_01HX...", "merchant_id": "smb_482", "type": "order.placed", "customer": { "phone": "+91...", "lang": "hi" }, "payload": { "order_id": "ord_...", "total": 1499 } }
Step 3 · Route

Event Classifier

A lightweight classifier decides: does this event need a voice call, an email, or nothing? Rule-based for the first 80% of events (order-placed → confirmation call). Small LLM (Haiku-class) for ambiguous cases. Latency budget: 200 ms per routing decision.

Step 4 · Model routing

Cost-Aware Model Selection

Simple confirmations run on smallest available model. Multi-turn conversations upgrade to larger models mid-call based on complexity heuristic (customer pushback, unusual query). Keeps inference cost at ₹2-4 per completed call vs ₹8-12 if everything used the premium model.

Step 5 · Execute

Voice + Email Agent

Voice agent dials customer (Exotel / Plivo for telephony). Uses customer context from POS (name, language, order history). Follows business-type script template. Every disclosure and customer response logged with timestamp.

Step 6 · Feedback loop

Reverse Webhook + Rating

After each interaction, GreyLabs POSTs outcome back to POS via reverse webhook. Owner sees it in their existing interface. Thumbs up / down from owner feeds the eval set — per-merchant golden sets emerge in 30-60 days.

10 — Product Flow

From POS Toggle to Live Agent

The SMB owner's journey — from the moment they see "Enable AI Voice Agent" inside Khatabook or Dukaan to their first autonomous call.

  1. Owner logs into POS. Sees a new toggle: "Enable GreyLabs AI Voice Agent" inside the customer-management section.
  2. Clicks. POS redirects to Connect's OAuth consent screen. Owner sees exactly which scopes are requested.
  3. Grants consent. Redirected back to POS with a success state. Connect receives scoped refresh token.
  4. Connect reads business category metadata from POS (clinic, retail, service). Auto-loads matching voice template — tone, script, disclosures.
  5. Owner is walked through a 2-minute setup: business name, voice persona (male/female, Hindi/English/Tamil), greeting sample.
  6. Owner makes a test call to their own agent. Hears the voice, confirms the script is right, adjusts the greeting if needed.
  7. Goes live. Next incoming event from POS fires the full flow. Agent handles the call autonomously.
  8. Owner gets a morning digest: calls handled, outcomes, cost, calls flagged for their review.

Time to First Call

Target: under 1 hour from OAuth click to first autonomous call. Compare to current enterprise onboarding of 4-6 weeks.

100x faster to value

14 — Prioritisation

What to Ship, in What Order

Quick wins unlock distribution. Strategic bets lock in the moat. Avoid the obvious traps.

Quick Wins — Low Effort, High Impact

  • Connector for top 3 POS — Khatabook, Dukaan, Paytm for Business. Biggest merchant coverage in one release. 6-8 weeks.
  • Templates for top 5 business types — clinic, retail, tuition, local service, hotel. Reusable, not bespoke.
  • Owner test call flow — non-negotiable for trust. 2 weeks of product work.
  • Morning digest email — daily outcome summary. Pure engagement loop.

Strategic Bets — High Effort, High Impact

  • Business-type auto-detection — small model classifies business from POS metadata + first 10 transactions. Removes the "pick your business type" step.
  • Per-merchant golden eval set — first 50 calls become that merchant's eval set. Quality stops drifting silently.
  • Voice persona customisation — female / male, tone, language mix. Drives stickiness.
  • WhatsApp Business integration — where Indian SMBs actually live. 10-12 week build.

Fill-ins — Lower Impact, Cheap

  • Cost-alert thresholds — let merchants cap daily spend.
  • Shareable transcript links — useful for small-team operations.
  • Referral credits — SMB-native viral loop.

Avoid — High Effort, Low Return

  • Marketplace of all POS on day one — 3 deep integrations beat 30 shallow ones.
  • White-label for BFSI clients — direct conflict with enterprise motion.
  • Building own POS — scope creep of the worst kind. Partner, never compete.
15 — Risks

What Could Kill This

Every strategic bet has a shape of failure. Name each risk, say how you mitigate, keep the honesty.

POS Partners Build Their Own Voice AI

Khatabook / Paytm could build a generic AI feature themselves. Mitigation: Move fast, lock in exclusive-ish partnerships with revenue share. Our models are already better. Become the default before they notice.

Channel risk

Enterprise Team Feels Deprioritised

BFSI revenue still pays the bills. Enterprise team could see SMB as a distraction. Mitigation: Separate P&L, separate leadership. Shared AI stack, independent go-to-market. Document the non-cannibalisation explicitly.

Internal risk

Unit Economics Collapse at Scale

Heavy SMB usage with no price levers = margin death. Mitigation: Usage-based pricing from day one. Model routing to keep inference cost down. Daily cost-per-merchant monitoring. Flat pricing is a trap.

Financial risk

TRAI / RBI Restrictions on Outbound AI Voice

India has strict DND rules. Automated outbound calls without consent are a landmine. Mitigation: Opt-in by the end customer at purchase time. Clear "this is an AI agent from <business>" disclosure. Honour DND registry. Log everything.

Regulatory risk

Quality Drift Across 10,000 Merchants

BFSI has 50 clients; eval is tractable. SMB is 10,000+; eval becomes a fire hose. Mitigation: Per-cohort golden eval sets. Automated LLM-as-judge on 5% sample. Human review only of flagged calls. Drift alerts at cohort level, not global.

Quality risk

SMBs Churn After Month One

Novelty wears off, retention fails. Mitigation: Activation ritual + morning digest keep daily engagement. Clear ROI number ("you saved ₹X and 4 hours this week") in the digest. Churn alerts fire at 21 days of declining usage.

Retention risk
