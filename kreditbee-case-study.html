02 — Problem

The Repayment Crisis

Defaults climb, recovery costs explode, and RBI's 2026 crackdown makes the old playbook illegal.

Scale

Defaults Climbing

Fintech personal loans hit ~3.6% overdue (90+ DPD) industry-wide. KreditBee at 2.8% — each basis point is crores in write-offs.

Cost

Ops Unsustainable

Fully loaded cost per agent: ₹31k–57k/month. Chasing micro-EMIs across millions of thin-file borrowers eats margin whole.

Regulation

RBI 2026 Rules

July 1: no calls after 7 PM, no threats, no family contact, certified agents only. Violations = fines, bans, delisting.

Brand

Trust Eroding

Forums flooded with harassment complaints — threats, abuse, one-day-late escalations. Users ghost instead of negotiate.

Case study question: How can KreditBee reduce defaults by 20%, cut recovery ops by 40%, and remain RBI-compliant — without sacrificing disbursement velocity?

03 — Users

Who Borrows — and Why They Don't Pay

Based on the 2022 FAccT paper "We can't find fault with a friend" and observed behavior patterns.

Largest segment

Gig Workers

Delivery, ride-hail, daily-wage. ₹15k one month, ₹8k the next. Borrow for emergencies. Default trigger: income dip.

High repeat

Small Merchants

Tier-2/3 shops, freelancers. Seasonal cash flow. Borrow for inventory. Default trigger: seasonal mismatch with rigid EMIs.

Multi-app risk

Young Salaried

Early-career, thin credit. Borrow for gadgets, weddings. Default trigger: over-borrowing across multiple apps.

Users see instant loans as a "miracle." They feel grateful, hand over data, contacts, dignity. When harassment begins, they blame themselves. Users don't default from laziness — they vanish from fear and shame.

06 — Competitive

Slice Pays, KreditBee Doesn't

Same demographics, similar products. The difference is system design, not user quality.

Slice — Reward-First

UPI credit card + split payments. Pay early → no interest, 3% cashback. Auto-pay gives discounts. Users feel rewarded for paying.

Data edge: Users transact via Slice UPI — real-time spending data. The Paytm advantage, built in.

KreditBee — Fear-First

Pure lending app. Take loan → pay EMI → get penalized. No transaction data. Recovery = calls, agents, threats. Users feel trapped.

Observation: Users who default on LazyPay/Simple pay Slice on time — incentive design makes paying feel better than defaulting.

KreditBee can't become Slice — no UPI ecosystem. But it can steal the principle: make repayment feel like a win. Close the data gap through partnerships, not by building a payments app.

07 — Solution

Bucketed Recovery

Different DPD buckets need different interventions. One-size recovery is why ops costs explode.

0–30 DPD

Nudge & Automate

AI voice + SMS. Auto-debit retry. One-tap UPI. Social proof. Low cost, high volume.

30–60 DPD

Vault + Smart Scoring

Auto-deduct vault. AA cash-flow check. Partial pay. AI with sentiment detection.

60–90 DPD

Consequence Engine

Real-time bureau update. Salary-link deduction. Settlement: 70% now. Certified agent last resort.

90+ DPD

Legal / Write-off

Auto-file e-courts. Portfolio sale to ARCs. Permanent credit flag. Cap the bleed.

The shift: "same pipeline for everyone" → bucketed, escalating, cost-appropriate. AI handles 70–80% at ₹3–8/call. Humans touch 20–30% — where urgency matters.

08 — Feature

The Vault

Advance EMI buffer for repeat borrowers and past defaulters. Absorbs the first missed payment.

  1. User applies. System checks history. Past default or 3+ loans → Vault triggered.
  2. Approval screen: "Deposit 1 month's EMI into your Vault to access this loan."
  3. User deposits via UPI. Held in escrow. Loan disbursed.
  4. If EMI missed → Vault auto-deducts. "We covered this month from your vault."
  5. Loan completed, zero misses → Vault returned with 2% bonus.

Zero Ops on First Miss

No call, no agent, no cost. The vault absorbs silently.

~₹500–2000 saved/case

Skin in the Game

Loss aversion — users who deposit upfront default less. Walking away means losing the vault.

Reduces casual defaulters

Loyalty Loop

Vault bonus on clean completion. Repeat borrowers stay for rewards, not fear.

Retention play

RBI-Safe

Disclosed upfront, consent-based, refundable. Within NBFC guidelines.

Regulatory green
09 — Feature

Credit Consequence Engine

Systemic urgency without a phone call. At 60 DPD, future borrowing everywhere evaporates — visibly.

  1. At 60 DPD, push real-time delinquency to CIBIL/Experian/CRIF. Accelerated, not monthly batch.
  2. In-app: "Your credit access across 47+ platforms is now restricted."
  3. Notification: "Your delinquency is visible to all lenders. Pay ₹X to restore."
  4. Pay within 15 days → "cured" update. Score recovers.
  5. Multi-app borrowers feel this hardest — can't borrow elsewhere to cover.

Systemic, Not Personal

No agent, no call. The entire credit ecosystem responds.

Zero ops cost

Kills Multi-App Arbitrage

Strategic defaulters rely on intact scores. Accelerated reporting breaks the game.

Highest behavioral impact

Bureau Call: ₹2–5

vs. ₹500–2000 per agent chase. More urgency than any call — real, visible, permanent.

API integration only
10 — Feature

Salary-Link Deduction

Bypass user choice. Pre-consented payroll deduction at 60+ DPD.

  1. At onboarding, users consent: "In default beyond 60 days, we may request your employer to deduct EMI."
  2. Gig workers: consent to deduction from Swiggy/Zomato/Ola payouts via partner API.
  3. At 60 DPD, automated request sent. Formal request with prior consent.
  4. EMI deducted at source before user receives salary.
  5. User notified: "EMI adjusted from your next payout per signup consent."

Bypasses Behavior

Money moves before the user sees it. Removes the option to ghost.

Highest recovery certainty

Gig Platform Synergy

Swiggy/Zomato benefit too — financially stable workers churn less.

Partnership required

Deterrent Effect

The knowledge alone changes behavior. 3–4× more likely to self-cure before 60 DPD.

Behavioral nudge

Pre-consented deduction is permissible under India's Payment of Wages Act and RBI digital lending guidelines. Employer cooperation is voluntary. Consent + notification keeps this compliant.

12 — Prioritization

Effort vs. Impact

Ship quick wins first. Build strategic bets in parallel.

Quick Wins — Low Effort, High Impact

  • Credit Consequence Engine — Bureau API. Days to build, immediate behavioral shift.
  • AI-first 0–30 DPD — Vendor integration. Replaces 70% of early calls. 2–4 weeks.
  • Bucketed escalation — Config change. Stop blanket-calling. Immediate savings.

Strategic Bets — High Effort, High Impact

  • Vault — Escrow + partner bank. 2–3 months. Changes default economics.
  • Salary-link — Employer/gig partnerships. 3–6 months. Highest certainty.
  • AA scoring — Account Aggregator for underwriting. Prevents over-lending.

Fill-ins — Low Effort, Lower Impact

  • Social proof — "92% in your area paid." 1-sprint test. Near-zero cost.
  • Repayment simulator — "₹10k = ₹800/mo" at onboarding. Reduces impulse.
  • Partial pay — "Pay what you can." Simple but moral hazard risk.

Avoid — High Effort, Low Impact

  • Building own UPI — Paytm/PhonePe own this. Partner, don't compete.
  • Full AI recovery — Can't create urgency for conniving 60+ DPD users.
  • Gamified debt loops — Badges without real value. Users see through it.
13 — Risks

What Could Go Wrong

Every solution has an edge. Where these get uncomfortable — and how to mitigate.

Vault Kills Conversion

Advance deposit adds friction. Urgent users may abandon. Mitigation: Only past defaulters + high-frequency. A/B test first.

Conversion risk

Salary-Link Feels Invasive

Users may see employer contact as shaming. Mitigation: Frame as convenience. Employer never sees default details.

PR risk

Bureau Reporting — Legal Gray

Accelerated push may face bureau pushback. Mitigation: Partner on "fast-track" program. Position as transparency.

Regulatory edge

Settlement Moral Hazard

"70%, fees waived" trains strategic defaults. Mitigation: One per lifetime, flagged in bureau. Second = straight to legal.

Behavioral risk

Every urgency lever risks feeling aggressive. Every comfort lever risks enabling abuse. The job is to calibrate precisely — by DPD bucket, user history, and real-time signals.

14 — Summary

The Playbook

Stop treating recovery as a single pipeline. Bucket by DPD, match intervention to intent, build consequence into the system — not into phone calls.
